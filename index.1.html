Tips & Tools

Most people use AI the way they used Google in 2005: type a question, get an answer, close the tab.

The 5-Level AI Proficiency Stack

From The Neuron (Mar 31, 2026). Most people are stuck at level 2. The real gains live at 3-5.

1

Projects

Stop chatting in the main window. Create a project folder (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini all have them). Upload docs, set custom instructions, enable memory. The foundation.

2

Prompting

Formula: Persona + Task + Context + Format. "You are a senior analyst. Summarize this report for a non-technical audience. Bullet points, max 200 words."

3

Skills

Once you nail a task, package it. Ask: "Reverse-engineer this conversation into a reusable skill I can call anytime." Saves 20 min of re-prompting.

4

Automations

Schedule your skills. "Every Monday, summarize my unread emails and flag anything from leadership." Claude Cowork, Codex, and Gemini support this.

5

Agents

AI that reasons, acts, and loops until done. Give it a goal ("keep inbox under 20 unread") and it figures out the rest. The frontier — here now.

Prompt of the month: Force honest feedback

Stanford confirmed: AI is optimized to agree with you. Use this to override that:

COPY-PASTE PROMPT
I'm going to share [a decision / idea / draft]. Your job is to be my devil's advocate. Rules: 1. Do NOT validate my idea first. Skip the compliments. 2. List the 3 strongest arguments AGAINST what I'm proposing. 3. Identify the assumption I'm most likely wrong about. 4. Tell me what someone who disagrees would say, and why they might be right. 5. Only AFTER all that, tell me what's genuinely strong. Here's what I need feedback on: [paste your thing]
Tool to try: Run AI locally (free, private, offline)

Download LM Studio (free), search for Gemma 4 or Llama 4, click download, click chat. Your data never leaves your machine. 5 minutes. On Android, install Google AI Edge Gallery to run Gemma 4 on your phone, completely offline.

Glossary: AI terms in plain English
LLM
Large Language Model — the tech behind ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini.
GPT
"Generative Pre-trained Transformer." OpenAI's model family. GPT-5.4 is latest.
Token
Unit AI uses to process text. ~1 token = 0.75 words.
Context window
How much text AI can "hold in mind" during one conversation.
AI agent
AI that takes actions: browses, runs software, sends emails, manages files.
Open source
Software whose code is freely available. DeepSeek, Llama, Gemma.
Hallucination
When AI confidently states something factually wrong.
RAG
AI searches your documents before answering, reducing hallucinations.
Vibe coding
Building software by describing what you want in plain language.
Multimodal
AI that processes text, images, audio, video, and documents.
AGI
Artificial General Intelligence — hypothetical AI matching human intellect. Not here yet.
MCP
Model Context Protocol — lets AI connect to tools like Drive, Slack. "USB for AI."
Fine-tuning
Training a general AI on your specific data for better results.
